留下 1129 是杭州一处社区中心——一个被日常与光线包裹的公共空间。镜头在这里放慢脚步，记录建筑与光影的秩序、人与空间安静而持久的对话，以及时间里缓缓流动的细节。

Liuxia 1129 is a community center in Hangzhou — a public space wrapped in everyday life and light. The lens slows down here to record the order of architecture and shadow, the quiet, enduring dialogue between people and space, and the small details drifting through time.

About · 关于
